| T-02-02 | Information Disclosure | seller UUID / secret leaking into logs/external-calls.jsonl | mitigate | logged_call never receives the UUID from callers; test_logging.py::test_no_secret_in_log asserts the UUID prefix is absent; logs/ gitignored; test_env.py scans src/+tests/ for the literal |
